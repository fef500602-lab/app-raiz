## Brand & Style

The design system channels the grounded power, ancestral memory, and kinetic joy of traditional Capoeira. It reflects an aesthetic that is organic, rhythmic, and authentic—rooted in the red clay of Bahia, the deep foliage of Mata Atlântica, and the communal warmth of the roda. 

The emotional signature balances respect for tradition with athletic discipline: warm, welcoming, and charged with energy. Rather than adopting sterile modern minimalism or glossy tech clichés, the interface draws from woodcut prints (literatura de cordel), hand-carved berimbaus, and sun-baked earth. Visual treatments emphasize tactile surfaces, rhythmic vertical cadence, and natural pigment contrasts.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from Afro-Bahian soil, vegetation, and solar radiance:

- **Primary (Terracotta Earth - `#C85A32`, `#D9653B`):** Represents clay, iron-rich earth, and athletic passion. Used for prominent calls to action, active movement states, and key navigational highlights.
- **Secondary (Palm Foliage Green - `#1E4B38`, `#2E6B4F`):** Evokes the resilience of coastal forests and Quilombo sanctuaries. Used for grounding elements, progression checkpoints, and instructional headers.
- **Tertiary (Bahia Sun & Amber - `#E5A93C`, `#F4C430`):** Expresses solar energy, musical bells (caxixi/agogô), and mastery badges. Used for accent badges, streak highlights, and subtle borders.
- **Neutral Backgrounds (Warm Sand & Linen - `#FAF6F0`, `#F3ECE2`):** A soft, tactile, low-eyestrain backdrop that feels like natural fiber or unbleached canvas.
- **Dark Neutral & Text (Soil Charcoal - `#1A1615`, `#241E1C`):** Replaces pure digital black with deep roasted soil tones to preserve organic warmth across all typography and solid surfaces.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the bold, rhythmic, hand-crafted forms of Bricolage Grotesque with the legible, friendly humanism of Plus Jakarta Sans. 

- **Display & Headings (Bricolage Grotesque):** Used for ritual section titles, movement names (Ginga, Meia Lua, Aú), songs, and capoeirista ranks. Its expressive character evokes woodcut typography and Afro-Brazilian folk prints.
- **Body & Functional UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for instructional sequencing, historical texts, and metadata. Clean apertures and balanced metrics ensure legibility during motion analysis or fast scanning in outdoor daylight.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adheres to a flexible 4-column structure on mobile devices, expanding to 8 columns on tablet devices:

- **Mobile Viewports (< 600px):** Single-column content stack with `16px` screen-edge padding and `16px` gutters. Critical movement sequences utilize horizontal snap carousels to preserve vertical flow.
- **Tablet & Landscape (600px - 1024px):** Dual-pane layout pairing sequence breakdown/video feeds on the left with contextual chords, history, and musical lyrics (ladainhas) on the right. Margins increase to `24px`.
- **Rhythm & Cadence:** Vertical spacing follows an 8px base grid, creating a predictable cadence that mirrors the syncopated timing of the pandeiro and atabaque.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through warm, low-opacity ambient shadows paired with layered linen-tone surfaces, eschewing artificial high-blur dropshadows:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#FAF6F0` linen surface.
- **Layer 1 (Cards & Modules):** Tinted warm sand `#F3ECE2` with a subtle 1px border (`rgba(26, 22, 21, 0.06)`) and an ambient tinted shadow: `0px 4px 16px rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Action & Overlays):** `#FAF6F0` elevated by `0px 8px 24px rgba(26, 22, 21, 0.12)`, topped with a hairline border in `#E5A93C` at 30% opacity.
- **Layer 3 (Modal Sheets & Draggables):** Grounded by a dark soil wash overlay (`rgba(26, 22, 21, 0.6)`) with `16px` top rounded corners and an earthy, non-blurry cast shadow.

## Shapes

The design system employs a consistent, organic curvature (level `2`), giving surfaces a friendly, carved touch without looking overly synthetic or pillowy:

- Default components, cards, and container panels use an explicit `16px` radius (`rounded-lg`).
- Interactive inputs, buttons, and alert modules follow `12px` to `16px` radii.
- Circular treatment is reserved exclusively for player avatars, berimbau tuning pins, and circular footstep anchor points in sequence diagrams.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Terracotta `#C85A32` with `#FAF6F0` text, `16px` radius, `48px` minimum touch height, subtle bottom shadow tinted in `#1A1615`.
- **Secondary Action:** Palm Foliage `#1E4B38` fill or 1.5px solid border on `#F3ECE2` sand container.
- **Artisanal Accent Button:** Bahian Amber `#E5A93C` with dark charcoal `#1A1615` text, used for graduation milestones and special batizado events.

### Cards & Movement Panels
- Built on sand/linen `#F3ECE2` with a `16px` border radius and `1px` subtle outline in `rgba(26, 22, 21, 0.07)`.
- Video cards incorporate integrated pill indicators in Bahia Amber displaying speed (e.g., "0.5x", "Slow-Mo") and cordel-inspired line motifs framing footwork diagrams.

### Chips & Ranks
- Movement difficulty chips utilize deep palm green tints with uppercase `label-sm` text.
- Cordão (belt) indicators appear as tactile braided pills with a left-aligned dual-color accent circle mimicking authentic capoeira cords.

### Bottom Navigation Bar
- Fixed mobile navigation anchored with sand-off-white background `#FAF6F0` and a crisp `1px` upper border in `#F3ECE2`.
- Active items use Terracotta `#C85A32` iconography with a gentle terracotta glow dot beneath; inactive items rest in muted soil charcoal `#1A1615` at 50% opacity.
- Primary icons: Movement Sequences (footsteps/swirl), Video Archive (camera/play), Songs & Lore (cordel scroll/book).

### Inputs & Selection Controls
- Text inputs rest on `#FAF6F0` with a `1.5px` border in `#1E4B38` (20% opacity when idle, 100% when active).
- Checkboxes and radios adopt hand-drawn square-soft silhouettes with terracotta fill and off-white check markings.
